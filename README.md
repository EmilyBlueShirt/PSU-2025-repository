## Project Overview

This repository documents my Summer 2025 undergraduate research at Penn State University under Dr. Andrea P. Argüelles. I participated through both Penn State’s Summer Research Opportunities Program (SROP) and the Acoustical Society of America’s Summer Undergraduate Research or Internship Experience in Acoustics (SURIEA).

My project focused on ultrasonic characterization in fused silica, with emphasis on designing custom fixtures for shear-wave immersion testing. To support this work, I used CAD modeling, 3D printing, mathematical modeling, and computational analysis to develop fixture geometries for transducer alignment and wave propagation experiments.
